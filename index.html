📍 Garden State Golf Tradition

🌲 Pine Barrens, Skyline Views & Coastal Dunes

New Jersey is one of the most underrated powerhouses in world golf architecture. The state features three distinct geographic golf zones:

  • The Pine Barrens: Sandy terrain and natural scrub pine forests created Pine Valley—consistently ranked the #1 golf course on planet Earth.
  • New York Harbor & Skylines: World-class urban engineering like Liberty National and Bayonne turned industrial waterfronts into championship venues.
  • South Jersey Linksland: Coastal sand deposits allowed architects to build deep dune links layouts like Twisted Dune and Atlantic City Country Club.
