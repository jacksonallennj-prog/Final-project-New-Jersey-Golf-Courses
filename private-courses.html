1. Pine Valley Golf Club

Consistently ranked the #1 golf course on planet Earth. Surrounded by unpaved scrub wastes and thick pine forests.

Architect: A.W. Tillinghast | Rank: PGA Major Host

3. Somerset Hills Country Club

Architect: A.W. Tillinghast | Rank: Top 50 Worldwide

Divided into an open front nine and a dramatic wooded back nine featuring natural water hazards and undulating greens.

A masterclass in Donald Ross green complex design. Frequently hosts PGA Tour FedExCup playoff events.

5. Ridgewood Country Club

Architect: A.W. Tillinghast | Rank: 27-Hole Classic

Features three nine-hole loops anchored by a iconic French Normandy-style clubhouse built in 1929.

6. Baltusrol Golf Club (Upper Course)

Architect: A.W. Tillinghast | Rank: Major Host

Architect: Walter Travis | Rank: Jersey Shore Classic

Renowned for its expansive bunker complexes and subtle green slopes located just blocks from the Atlantic Ocean.
